An Elegant Evening

Escoffier Duck Confit

The Main Course

  • Features a masterfully crisped skin that crackles upon the first bite, highlighting the rich, rendered fat.
  • The succulent meat is slow-roasted for hours, resulting in a perfectly tender, fall-off-the-bone texture.
  • Ideally served hot over a bed of roasted potatoes or paired with a sturdy, bitter chicory salad.

Chocolate Macarons

The Sweet Treats

  • Feathery-light almond meringue shells infused with rich cocoa for a delicate yet satisfying crunch.
  • Filled with a decadent, bittersweet ganache made from high-quality chopped chocolate and heavy cream.
  • Carefully aged for 24 hours to ensure a melt-in-the-mouth consistency and a deeply balanced chocolate profile.

Crepes with Raspberry-Cassis Sauce

The Dessert Specialty

  • Delicate, hand-turned crepes wrapped around a zesty honey-lime Greek yogurt filling for a creamy core.
  • Generously drizzled with a luxurious raspberry-cassis sauce infused with fragrant notes of rose water.
  • A light, floral, and refreshing specialty that serves as a graceful finale to our gastronomic journey.
